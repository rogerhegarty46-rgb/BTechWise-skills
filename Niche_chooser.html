Niche

Turn the strengths you uncovered into a clear idea of who you could help and what problem you could solve.

In Weeks 1 and 2 you uncovered your hidden skills and reviewed your experience. This worksheet is the next step: it takes those strengths and helps you shape them into a focused niche — a simple statement of who you help and what you do for them. This week we are thinking about trends, so it also helps you check whether real demand exists before you commit your time.

★ There are no right or wrong answers — answer as honestly as you can, and the result will be more useful.
HOW IT WORKS – answer the steps below, then build your niche statement and an AI-ready prompt.
1

Bring your strengths across from your Skills Review, then answer a few simple steps about who and what.

2

We turn your answers into a draft niche, some worked examples, and a quick way to check demand.

3

Copy the AI prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to sharpen it into three realistic options.
