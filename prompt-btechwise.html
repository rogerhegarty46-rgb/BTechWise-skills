Meet Prompt

Your personal skills memory guide

Prompt is going to take you on a gentle journey through your past — asking questions about things you've done, helped with, organised, or achieved across different areas of your life. The goal is simply to help you remember experiences that reveal real skills, many of which you may not even realise you have.

  • There are no right or wrong answers — just talk naturally
  • Nothing is too small or too ordinary to mention
  • No formal language needed — just say it as it comes
  • At the end, Prompt gives you a full summary ready for your Skills Assessment

A gentle reminder

Think broadly as you go. Skills come in all shapes — including things people often overlook. Things like using Word, Excel, or spreadsheets regularly. Managing a household budget, club finances, or charity accounts. Using apps, email, video calls, social media, or online tools day to day. Helping someone else with their phone or computer. These are all real, valuable skills. If it crosses your mind, mention it — no experience is too everyday to count.
