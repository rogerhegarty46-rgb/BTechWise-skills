Your Experience & Strength Review

Discover what you already have — and where it could take you

This worksheet helps you uncover strengths and experience you may not even realise you have. This is for you. Fill it in on your computer, tablet, or phone — then use the summary tool at the bottom to copy your answers straight into an AI like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.


A simple format works best: Skill + Confidence score + a real example.
Example: Organising (Confidence 4) – Coordinated weekly rotas for a local group of 8 people.


List as many skills as you like in each section — there is no limit. The more real your examples, the better your AI suggestions will be.

🎯 Confidence Score Guide

5 I enjoy this and feel confident doing it
4 I'm comfortable and capable
3 I can do it, but it takes effort
2 I've done it, but it's not my strength
1 I would rather avoid this

Your 4s and 5s highlight strengths to build on. Your 1s and 2s help the AI steer you away from paths you wouldn't enjoy — so be honest!
