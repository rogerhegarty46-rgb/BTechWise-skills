Your Hidden
Skills Checklist

A simple worksheet to help you identify valuable experience, talents, and interests before completing the Skills Evaluation Questionnaire.

Many people underestimate the knowledge and abilities they have built up over a lifetime. A retired nurse, RAF engineer, taxi driver, parent, volunteer, gardener, or hobby photographer may not think of themselves as having "business skills", yet each has developed valuable experience that others may find useful. This worksheet is designed to help you uncover those hidden strengths and see them in a new light.

★ There are no right or wrong answers, and no skill is too small to include.
RATING GUIDE – For each skill or experience that applies to you, give yourself a score from 1 to 5.
1

I can do this, but I would not choose to do it regularly.

2

I can do this and would not mind doing it occasionally.

3

I am reasonably competent and would be happy to do it if it provided worthwhile income.

4

I am confident and capable, although it is not something I am especially passionate about.

5

I am highly confident in this area and would genuinely enjoy doing it again.

Final Thought

Many successful self-employed people build businesses around skills they once considered "ordinary". The purpose of the Skills Evaluation Questionnaire is to identify opportunities that match both your abilities and your interests. The more accurately you describe your skills, experiences, and preferences, the more useful and personalised your results are likely to be.

You probably know more than you think! 🙂

Keeping Your Results

1. Save your progress. Use the Save button to keep a copy on your computer, tablet or phone. You can reopen it later with the Load button to review or carry on.

2. No printer? Make a PDF instead. Press the Print button and, where it asks which printer to use, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Print, then share or save the preview as a PDF.

Most modern computers, tablets and phones can do this — so you can keep a tidy copy even without a printer.
